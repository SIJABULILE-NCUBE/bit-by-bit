## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a warm, profoundly trustworthy, and accessible financial experience tailored for South African informal and community retail merchants and their shoppers. The visual tone departs from sterile, corporate neo-banking, leaning instead into approachable financial empowerment—celebrating patience, dignity, and debt-free ownership.

Key aesthetic characteristics:
- **Style Archetype:** Modern Tactile Fintech. Combines clean, high-clarity typography with warm, comforting material depth, generous proportions, and high-contrast affordances designed for outdoors and varied lighting conditions.
- **Physicality & Confidence:** Substantial touch targets, reassuring tap feedback, soft elevated cards, and deliberate spacing communicate reliability and transparency.
- **Audience & Context:** Small shop owners (spaza shops, boutique apparel, hardware, community retail) and local shoppers making manageable lay-by progress. Layouts prioritize zero visual ambiguity, explicit currency readability, and immediate verification.

## Colors

The color palette centers on financial prosperity, community growth, and crystal-clear status communication.

- **Primary (`#0F5132` - Deep Forest Green):** Anchors main actions, headers, identity statements, and verified completion states. Signifies stability, deep roots, and ethical wealth accumulation.
- **Secondary (`#F5B700` - Radiant Warm Gold):** Reserved for accent triggers, milestone achievements, progress trackers, and high-impact micro-engagements. Evokes value and celebratory milestones without resembling hazard alerts.
- **Tertiary (`#2B825B` - Leaf Green):** Functions as supporting interactive states, progress fill bars, and active badge backgrounds.
- **Neutral Tone System:**
  - Canvas Background: Warm Cream/Off-white (`#F8F9FA` to `#F4F6F4`), minimizing glare under harsh sunlight.
  - Surface Containers: Pure crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) to ensure maximum legibility against the tinted canvas.
  - Neutral Base / Text: Deep Olive Slate (`#1E2421`) for high readability without the harshness of pure black.
- **Semantic Status Tiers:**
  - *Confirmed / Completed:* Emerald (`#0F5132` / `#10B981`)
  - *Pending / Processing:* Warm Amber (`#D97706` / `#FEF3C7`)
  - *Rejected / Overdue:* Rich Rose (`#BE123C` / `#FFE4E6`)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the friendly, organic geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and brand expressions with the functional neutrality and precision of **Inter** for data tables, amounts, and dense body text.

- **South African Rand (ZAR) Formatting:** All monetary sums must employ tabular numbers (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1`) and non-breaking spaces for South African notation: `R 1 250,00`. Currency symbols remain in semibold, while integer values take prominence to ensure instant cognitive assessment.
- **Hierarchy Enforcement:** Headline levels must never drop below 600 weight to maintain high visual impact over varied card backgrounds. Small captions and micro-labels utilize slight letter spacing for sharp rendering on lower-resolution mobile screens.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid layout that guarantees touch ergonomics and clean content separation:

- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column layout with fixed bottom navigation and sticky CTA zones. 16px lateral canvas margins maximize screen real estate while protecting content from hardware borders.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid structure with 20px gutters. Order summaries and payment plans sit side-by-side or stacked according to contextual importance.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Max container width of 1200px centered with a 12-column layout. Retail merchant management dashboards separate the live catalog from the customer lay-by tracking column.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Consistent multiples of 4px and 8px govern element stacks. Section boundaries use generous spacing (`space-xl` or `space-2xl`) to avoid visual fatigue during multi-step deposit workflows.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses layered warm surfaces paired with soft, low-intensity ambient shadows to build a tactile, reassuring interface:

- **Base Canvas:** `#F4F6F4` provides a grounded substrate.
- **Surface Tier 1 (Cards, Modules):** `#FFFFFF` with a crisp, low-opacity border (`rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.08)`) and an ambient tinted drop shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Tier 2 (Raised Sheets, Modals, Pickers):** Elevated surface featuring `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`.
- **Interactive Focus & Hover:** Button presses and card selections slightly diminish vertical elevation (`translateY(1px)`) with an immediate border highlight (`#F5B700` or `#0F5132`), confirming state change without jarring micro-animations.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a distinctly friendly, approachable geometry characterized by comfortable curves:

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Use `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) to present store goods, payment milestones, and lay-by status cards with soft edges.
- **Inputs & Action Buttons:** Use `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) to reinforce comfortable fingertip fit.
- **Badges, Tags, and Toggles:** Use full pill rounding (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to communicate modularity and easy readability.
- **Progress Trackers:** Segmented bars utilize fully rounded interior segments (8px) for an encouraging visual metaphor of filling up a physical goal jar.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Minimum Touch Target:** 48px height across all clickable elements, expanding to 54px on primary mobile bottom-sheet triggers.
- **Primary Button:** `#0F5132` solid fill, white text, 16px rounded corners, with semi-bold typography. Tap state scales down to 0.98.
- **Secondary / Accent Action:** `#F5B700` background with deep slate text (`#1E2421`), reserved for direct payment triggers ("Make a Deposit", "Claim Item").
- **Outline / Merchant Action:** 1.5px border of `#0F5132`, transparent background, dark green text.

### Status Badges
- Padded at `6px 12px` with full pill radius and uppercase label-sm typography:
  - **Pending:** `#FEF3C7` background with `#92400E` text and dot indicator.
  - **Confirmed / Paid:** `#DCFCE7` background with `#0F5132` text and checkmark indicator.
  - **Rejected / Lapsed:** `#FFE4E6` background with `#9F1239` text and warning indicator.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Height: 52px.
- Background: `#FFFFFF` encased in a 1px border (`#D1D5DB`).
- Focus state: Replaced by a 2px `#0F5132` border with a subtle warm glow (`0 0 0 4px rgba(245, 183, 0, 0.2)`).
- Prefix currency indicators (e.g., "R") are anchored with high-contrast text and a subtle right divider.

### Lay-by Progress Cards
- Pure white container with a 24px corner radius.
- Includes a prominent Rand total, item thumbnail, merchant name, installment timeline, and a dual-tone progress bar (filled in `#0F5132` with pending milestone indicators in `#F5B700`).

### Installment Milestone Stepper
- Horizontal progress visual depicting completed, active, and upcoming payment rounds.
- Each milestone node is at least 32px wide to clearly display checkmarks or step numbers, ensuring complete transparency on remaining balances.
